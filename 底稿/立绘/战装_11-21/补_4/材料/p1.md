# 山铜纪第一页 犁的年月（2到300年）：1 人

====================
【s1n_fatih】法提赫·焦门（m，man）
传奇
长相（英文资料）：Fatih Scorchgate of the flame-horn clan, the door-ram of Grey Ridge, a demon whose name set before the ninety-ninth city gate he broke; he has looked a little under thirty for centuries. Tall and broad-shouldered. White hair shaved close to the scalp, amber-red eyes. A pair of horns, each ringed by a band of black obsidian, the tips scorched black. On the back of his left hand (on the viewer's right when he faces us) ninety-nine small dot brands in neat rows, one place left empty. His sword Embergate, forged from gate nails, never leaves him. Slow, courteous and patient; he talks about everyth
常服：His everyday clothes in the western camps: a black leather short coat with the cuffs closed by iron rings, a dark grey coarse wool tunic underneath, a wide belt, dark wool trousers and heavy hide boots, a sheepskin mantle thrown over the right shoulder. In his right hand he holds the sword Embergate in a scabbard studded with old iron nail heads, a polite, patient look.
现在的战装（要改写的这一句）：The door-ram at a crossing: a heavy black leather coat with iron-ringed cuffs over a shirt of blackened iron scales, hide bracers, heavy boots. He holds the sword Embergate in both hands: a long straight single-edged blade, NOT a curved sword, forged from old gate nails, the round nail heads still showing in rows along its dark iron flat with embers glowing between them; a calm, courteous stare.

## 灰岗的门锤，一百年没有门可破的叩城者，法提赫·焦门（人物）
- 样子：看着不到三十岁，高，肩宽，白发剃得贴着头皮，琥珀红的眼睛。角上箍着一圈黑曜，角尖是焦的。左手手背上烙着九十九个小点，一扇门一个，第一百个位置空着（★）。
- 做派：他说话慢，客气，三句不离门。
