长相（英文资料）：Winda, an old human midwife of the foot of the Crown Peak, eighty: about 156 cm, small and thin, with a slight stoop. Snow-white hair rolled into a bun at the back and held with one bone hairpin. A deeply lined face, yet her eyes are a bright new-leaf green, like the first leaf of spring. In her youth she was the most beautiful girl in the foothills, and she still shows it when she smiles. Small, dry, very clean hands with nails cut short; hands that have not trembled in sixty-five years. A calm, kind, steady air; a mother who looks at her is no longer afraid.
常服：Her midwife's clothes: a long indigo-blue coarse cloth dress to the ankles with long sleeves, a clean white apron whose pocket holds a pair of small silver scissors and a roll of boiled linen; on her back a wicker willow box of herbs, a hot-water flask and a small oil lamp. A calm, kind look.
