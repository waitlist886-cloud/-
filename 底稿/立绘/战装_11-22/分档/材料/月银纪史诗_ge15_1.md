【ge15_della】黛拉（f，woman）
资料出处：月银纪史诗/第十五歌_日冕之晨.md:240
长相（英文资料）：Della, a human woodcutter and fire-keeper of the east gate of the Sun Crown's city, thirty years old and looking it: about 166 cm, sturdy and square-shouldered, with strong forearms and palms hardened by axe handles. Deep teal-blue hair in one long braid coiled on top of her head so sparks cannot catch it, light blue eyes that narrow when she looks at flame. A lean, narrow-chinned face with a faint old burn scar on her LEFT cheek (on the viewer's right when she faces us), from a fire when she was eight. Fair skin. A practical, steady, quietly proud air.
常服：Her woodcutter's clothes: a coarse undyed wool short-sleeved tunic to the hip over brown linen trousers, a thick leather apron burned through with several small holes, hide boots wrapped with thongs; a short hand axe at her belt on the right side. She stands square with one hand resting on the axe haft.
现在的战装（要改写的这一句）：Ready to hold the gate: the scorched leather apron over a studded leather jerkin, leather bracers, the braid pinned under a plain iron cap. She holds the short axe low in her right hand, the left hand open at her side; a steady, narrowed-eyed look.
原页里他那一条：
## 旅途同行：黛拉（月银纪·日冕之晨）
身份：东门第二老的守火人，劈柴劈了二十二年。她怕火，却劈得比谁都齐，说这堆火与烧死父母的火不同。她八岁从西坡逃来的经过写在背景条里。
装束：她穿粗布短衣、厚皮围裙，围裙烧着小洞，短斧别在腰上。
底座：人类，女，30岁｜共鸣（LVL 36）｜职业 守火人·短斧｜法门 东门齐柴守火法
能力：Special【焰踪之眼】(Epic，她看得见火的脚印，先知道一片火往哪烧)｜Passive【灼痕体质】(Rare，烧过的皮不怕烫，徒手捧得住烧红的炭)｜Art【东门齐柴守火法】(Rare·二阶，灰桥传火练法里拆出的交火一截，备柴、码柴、教学徒，没有法门)
兼修：【灰桥传火】(Legend，照欧德那一门，用的是东门老守火人传下的那一版。开局她还不会，3000年坠叶月接火以后，老人在火边当面教她。交火那一截她二十二年劈柴早已练熟，补上的是护火那一科，添柴的老法子学得浅)

【ge15_jarlin】嘉琳（f，woman）
资料出处：月银纪史诗/第十五歌_日冕之晨.md:297
长相（英文资料）：Jarlin, a human old hunter of the southern grain plains, sixty-nine years old: about 168 cm, lean and long-legged, still straight-backed, a woman who can walk from village to village in a day. Short hair that was wheat-coloured, now mostly white with streaks of wheat gold, cut ragged by her own hunting knife, as it always was. Pale amber eyes like dried wheat straw. Her freckles have turned to age spots and her face is deeply lined, with a long nose. Fair skin. She stands with one foot slightly pointed on the toe, as if about to run. A blunt, free, unbowed air.
常服：Her hunter's clothes: an old short plains hunter's jerkin over a worn long-sleeved shirt and trousers, leg wraps and soft boots; a short bow of the grain plains slung across her back, its limbs twice replaced; a small cloth pouch of wheat grains at her belt. She stands with one foot pointed on its toe, a hand on the bow string.
现在的战装（要改写的这一句）：Ready to shoot: the old hunter's jerkin laced tight, leather bracer on the left forearm, a quiver at the waist. She holds the short bow drawn halfway with an arrow nocked, eyes narrowed at the distance; a calm, hard look.
原页里他那一条：
## 旅途同行：嘉琳（月银纪·日冕之晨）
类别：相遇｜粮原的老猎手，五十年前陪墨勒请过人。
身份：粮原的老猎手，一天还走得完别人两天的路。她五十年前陪墨勒请人，回乡后种下墨勒麦，没有嫁人。粮原王征她打仗，她不去。粮原的兵烧过西坡的麦，西坡的兵烧过粮原的麦，她家的麦地烧过三回，她种了三回。听说诸王又要围东城，她背弓走了七天，来看守火的。
装束：她穿旧猎衣、绑腿和软靴，背着换过两回弓臂的短弓，腰袋里装着墨勒麦。
底座：人类，女，69岁｜极境（LVL 92）｜职业 老猎手·短弓｜法门 粮原弓术
能力：Special【神行之足】(Epic，脚下生风，一天跑得完别人三天的路，踏过的草叶不折)｜Passive【战斗直觉】(Epic，兽扑过来时身子先于脑子闪开)｜Art【粮原弓术】(Rare·五阶，沿用第十四歌里粮原猎户的那版)
兼修：【猎营同食驭犬术】(Rare，约2960年她家麦地头一回被烧，一个人守不住一片麦地，她跟粮原猎营的驯犬人学了驭犬，用一锅喂大的两条犬帮她围野猪、看地头。听哨围追那一截学全了，借犬鼻子那一阶没有练到)

【ge15_leo】莱奥（m，man）
资料出处：月银纪史诗/第十五歌_日冕之晨.md:343
长相（英文资料）：Leo, a human young knight of the royal house of the grassland kingdom, nineteen: about 178 cm, slender, long-legged with a horseman's strength, shoulders not yet fully filled out. Light chestnut short hair with one curl falling across the forehead, amber eyes (the colour of the royal house). A handsome, still boyish face with a faint stubble on the chin and a small tiger-tooth showing when he smiles. Fair skin. A bright, earnest, slightly shy air.
常服：His knight's kit: a light plate cuirass whose plates are branded with a running wind-maned horse, over a padded cloth tunic, leather leggings and riding boots; a short dagger at his left hip (on the viewer's right when he faces us). He holds a long lance upright at his side, its tassel of black horsehair. A bright, open smile.
现在的战装（要改写的这一句）：Ready to charge: a side-vented leather riding tunic with a light cuirass of boiled-leather plates each branded with a running wind-maned horse, guarding only the chest and forearms, leather leg guards, soft boots, a plain open helm of hardened leather held under his arm. The horsehair-tasselled lance is lowered forward in both hands, and the branded horses flicker faintly with wind-light; a firm, determined look.
原页里他那一条：
## 旅途同行：莱奥（月银纪·日冕之晨）
身份：骑术牧野第一、枪法一般的年轻骑士。他看见父王的兵烧麦，枪都拿不稳，却不肯让自己一队跟着烧。家里老人讲过三百多年前骑白马上天的人，王家的来路写在背景条里。
装束：他穿烫着风鬃马的轻甲，长枪系马鬃缨。父王给的短刀挂在腰间，他没用过。
底座：人类，男，19岁｜灵格（LVL 52）｜职业 骑士·长枪｜法门 天马坠锋骑枪术
能力：Special【天马之裔】(Legend，他骑上马快过风，冲锋时人和马像要离地)｜Passive【蓝血骑范】(Epic，牧野王家的骑范，稳得住他自己那一队)｜Art【天马坠锋骑枪术】(Epic·二阶，沿用第十一歌里牧野骑手传下的那版)
兼修：【祭火守夜护身祷法】(Rare，照欧德那一版。3000年坠叶月天快亮，他跟到东门，看见欧德抬头。守冠的头一冬，他在冠殿前点起一盆真火，跟欧德学火边的三段护身祷。他枪法一般，守在殿前又不肯伤人，这三段补的就是这个短板。学到第二段)
