【ge12_king】珠湾王（m，man）
资料出处：月银纪史诗/第十二歌_珠湾沉冠.md:557
长相（英文资料）：The old king of the Pearl Bay, sixty years old: about 183 cm, still broad in the chest from youth spent diving deepest of all the bay's princes. A full head of white hair with a faint ring-shaped pressed line round the crown of his head where a crown has sat for forty years. Eyes the clear shallow-water green of his son's. A lined, composed face, a calm, counting air.
常服：An old king's plain clothes: a long deep sea-blue wool robe closed with buttons, a silver chain across the chest, a white linen shirt collar, soft boots; no crown on his head. He stands with his hands clasped behind his back, a calm, counting look.
现在的战装（要改写的这一句）：Commanding from the harbour wall: a sleeveless sharkskin coat set with a few pearls over the blue robe, leather bracers, a plain short cloak; one hand raised palm out, the other at his side, no weapon. A grave, firm look.
原页里他那一条：
## 珠湾王（人物）
- 是谁：他是六十岁的珠湾王，冠上只镶珠，说兽的东西不该戴在人的头上。他每天傍晚上塔数船，潮来时点烽火求援，第七夜数完最后几船，把冠交给珀拉，自己回王宫坐在没有冠的王座上。
- 样子：他白发碧眼，年轻时是湾里潜得最深的王子，老了胸口还宽。摘下戴了四十年的冠，头顶露出一圈光秃秃的压痕。
- 做派：他说话不多，爱数船、数户、数人。他说，“你替这座城唱，这座城，今夜就是你的。”
