  - who：Calantia, an elf of the old forest's Watcher clan who has kept her watch for some thirteen thousand years: tall, about 174 cm, slim, with very long legs. Skin pale as someone who lives under the canopy. Long pale-green hair shading to silver, in one long braid hanging below the waist. Long pointed elf ears.   - who：Fridolin Sten, a human stone-carver's son from the east, seventeen years old and looks it: about 171 cm, sturdy in the shoulders and back from hauling stone, wholly covered by his clothes.
